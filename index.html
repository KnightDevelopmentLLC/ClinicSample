MediCare Clinic has been a cornerstone of healthcare in the community for over two decades, combining cutting-edge medicine with heartfelt compassion.

🏥

Our Story

Founded in 2004 by Dr. Arjun Mehta, MediCare started as a small general practice with a simple mission: deliver honest, effective healthcare to every patient who walks through our doors.

Today, we've grown into a multi-specialty clinic with 15+ doctors, state-of-the-art equipment, and an in-house diagnostic laboratory — but our values remain the same.
